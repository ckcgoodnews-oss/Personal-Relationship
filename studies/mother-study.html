A study for my own adjustment

My adult son is responsible for his choices. This study helps me examine my conduct, separate facts from fears, and strengthen trust through respectful communication. His participation is voluntary; he does not need to agree with every concern I have.

Use this for reflection, not as a checklist for monitoring him. Actual serious wrongdoing calls for appropriate spiritual help. Ordinary imperfections, differing preferences, and family arrangements need patience and fair discussion.

A conversation to begin repairing trust

I love you, and I have been afraid about your future. I realize that fear has sometimes led me to accuse you or threaten to involve the elders. I want to listen and treat you as an adult. We may disagree, but I want us to discuss actual concerns respectfully and make fair arrangements while we share a home.
